Privacy Policy

Ember processes only what it needs

Ember has no sign-up, ads or analytics. Voice, screen and conversation processing for the Ember assistant stays on your Mac. Only device identification hashes, license information and the app version needed for license and usage policy checks, plus logs you choose and consent to send, leave your Mac. This policy explains what is processed, why, for how long and where.

Only what is neededNames, emails and payment details are not received by Ember servers. Devices are identified only by hashes, not their original identifiers.
Logs only with consentSend Logs shows everything to be sent, redacts personal information and sends only with your consent. Logs are deleted after 30 days.
On your Mac onlySpeech recognition, responses, voice synthesis and screen analysis continue to run entirely on your device.

8Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time ask the Ember developer to let you access, correct or delete your personal information, or stop processing it. You can withdraw consent, including consent to send logs, at any time.

Send your request through the contact channelbelow. To help locate your records, include your license key or the receipt numbershown in the app after sending logs. After verifying your identity, we will act without delay and inform you of the result. A legal representative or authorized agent may also submit a request.

If information must be retained by law, or deleting it would prevent us from providing the license, we will explain why. In that case, you can also choose to deactivate the license.

You can view and delete records left on your Mac yourself, and revoke permissions at any time.

9Security measures

Voice, screen and conversations are designed to stay on your Mac. Devices are identified only by hashes, not original identifiers. Communication with the server is encrypted with HTTPS; licenses and usage policies are applied only after their digital signatures are verified.

Personal information in logs is redacted on your Mac before sending. The server keeps the received logs compressed and deletes them after 30 days. Server access logs do not record IP addresses. Administration requires the Ember developer’s signing key, and administrative actions are logged.

Downloaded AI models are checked against fixed versions and file hashes before use. The Ember assistant’s advanced response engine listens only on this Mac (127.0.0.1) and cannot be accessed externally.
